FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else, give us a call on 0493 866 368.

How do you work out the price?

By how much of our trailer your rubbish fills, from $550 for a ¼ load up to $1,750 for a full load (about 16 m³). Mattresses are $120 extra and gas bottles $30, and garden waste on its own is 20% off. We confirm your final price on site before we start loading.

Are you insured?

Yes. Clean Sweep is fully insured, so you can relax while we work on your property.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If the rubbish is easy to get to, like on the driveway or in an unlocked shed, we can collect it while you're out. We'll send you a photo when it's done.

How soon can you come?

Get in touch and we'll give you the next available time. We're often able to come within a day or two.

What happens to my rubbish?

We sort it before it goes anywhere. Metal, cardboard and e-waste go to recycling, usable items can go to charity, and only what's left goes to a licensed waste facility.

Do you carry heavy items down stairs?

Yes. We do all the lifting from anywhere on the property, including upstairs, backyards and under the house. Stairs or a long carry can add a little to the price, and we'll tell you upfront.

How can I pay?

When you book online you can pay a $100 deposit or the full price through our secure Stripe checkout, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or direct debit from your bank account. Anything left over is settled on the day.
